## About the course

This course uses Harbour Home Supplies, a fictional SME, to connect content policies, file permissions, document creation, team communication, website maintenance, reporting and add-on evaluation. Activities use synthetic records rather than customer or employee data. Administrator features have documented demonstration or simulation routes when learner accounts lack access.

## Learning outcomes

- Implement Google Workspace content management policies and post and update content on Google Drive.
- Create and curate content, maintain web properties and assets, and compare relevant content management offerings.
- Report performance metrics, recommend suitable add-ons, and propose measurable improvements to customer and visitor experience.

## Topics covered

- **Topic 1 — Governance and Drive content lifecycle** (Activities 1–5): identity, ownership, effective permissions, classification, publication, version history and recovery.
- **Topic 2 — Content assets and customer web properties** (Activities 6–21): Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms, Gmail, Calendar, Meet, Chat, Sites and CMS comparisons.
- **Topic 3 — Metrics, add-ons and integration decisions** (Activities 22–25): defined performance measures, visitor funnels, Marketplace reviews, permission scopes and guarded Apps Script.

Jamboard's legacy collaboration material is replaced with currently supported collaboration activities; Jamboard is not required.
